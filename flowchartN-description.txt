Process: Morning routine

1. Start.
2. Input alarm_clock.
3. Go to sleep.
4. Output alarm_clock rings.
5. Wake-up.
6. Put clothes on.
7. Put water in a pot to boil.
8. Decision: Is the water boiling?
   - No: Wait 30 seconds, then check again.
   - Yes: Drop in 2 eggs and wait 6 minutes.
9. Put the eggs in cold water for 2 minutes.
10. Peel the shell and eat the egg.
11. Brush teeth.
12. Leave the house and lock the door.
13. End.
